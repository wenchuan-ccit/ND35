Summary of Common Calculus Techniques

Differentiation and integration are the foundation of engineering mathematics. This guide provides an interactive learning experience to help you quickly master the core techniques.

Part 1: Differentiation

The core of differentiation is finding the 'derivative' of a function, which represents the instantaneous rate of change or the slope of the tangent line at a specific point. It is an indispensable tool for analyzing the dynamic behavior of systems.

Basic Differentiation Formulas

$$\frac{d}{dx}(x^n) = nx^{n-1}$$
$$\frac{d}{dx}(\sin x) = \cos x$$
$$\frac{d}{dx}(\cos x) = -\sin x$$
$$\frac{d}{dx}(\tan x) = \sec^2 x$$
$$\frac{d}{dx}(e^x) = e^x$$
$$\frac{d}{dx}(\ln x) = \frac{1}{x}$$

Part 2: Integration

Integration can be seen as the inverse operation of differentiation, mainly used to calculate the 'total accumulation' during a process of change, such as the area under a curve, the volume of an object, or the total displacement over time.

Basic Integration Formulas

$$\int x^n \,dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1} + C$$
$$\int \cos x \,dx = \sin x + C$$
$$\int \sin x \,dx = -\cos x + C$$
$$\int e^x \,dx = e^x + C$$
$$\int \frac{1}{x} \,dx = \ln|x| + C$$
